Two minutes. As many sums as you can.

Zetamac is a speed drill for mental arithmetic. You get 120 seconds of addition, subtraction, multiplication and division, and your score is how many you get right.

  • InterviewsQuant and trading interviews often include a timed arithmetic test like this. Many people training for them aim for 80+.
  • SpeedDaily rounds make the common facts automatic, so you stop working out 7 × 8 and just know it.
  • One numberEvery day ends with one honest score to beat tomorrow.

Three games. Every question timed.

Pick a game and a length on its start screen, then press Enter. Every question is timed, so the tracker can see where your time goes.

  • ArithmeticZetamac’s default settings. 2:00 is your score; a 0:30 round counts ×4; Endless has no clock and records seconds per problem.
  • SquaresTwo-digit or three-digit squares, with a hard mode that skips the easy ones.
  • PracticeDrills for one kind of problem, like subtraction with borrowing. Untimed by default.
  • GuidedAn arithmetic option that shows what to picture in your head for each problem. Kept apart from your real scores.
