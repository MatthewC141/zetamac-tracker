Two minutes. As many sums as you can.

Zetamach is a speed drill for mental arithmetic. You get 120 seconds of addition, subtraction, multiplication and division, and your score is how many you get right.

  • InterviewsQuant and trading interviews often include a timed arithmetic test like this. Many people training for them aim for 80+.
  • SpeedDaily rounds make the common facts automatic, so you stop working out 7 × 8 and just know it.
  • One numberEvery day ends with one honest score to beat tomorrow.

Six games. Every question timed.

Pick a game and a length on its start screen, then press Enter. Every question is timed, so the tracker can see where your time goes.

  • ArithmeticZetamac’s default settings. 2:00 is your score; a 0:30 round counts ×4; Endless has no clock. Plus a daily challenge: the same questions for everyone, one try a day.
  • SquaresTwo-digit or three-digit squares, skipping the easy ones: nothing ending in 0 or 5, and no 1–20.
  • CombinedTwo steps in one problem, like (5 + 2) × (15 + 9): work out the brackets, then the rest.
  • TestsTrading-firm tests marked right minus wrong: 80 in 8 (Optiver-style), number sequences, fractions and estimation.
  • DuelRace another player on the same questions, ranked or for fun: find an opponent, challenge a friend by name, or race a ghost.
  • PracticeDrills for one kind of problem, including one built from your own weak spots. Untimed by default.
  • GuideThe quickest way to do each operation in your head, step by step, plus a times-table drill. Open Guide from the header.
